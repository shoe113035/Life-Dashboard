🧴 Skincare Routine

🧘 Daily Stretch & Strengthen Guide

Pick one each day
⚡ 5-min morning: cat-cow · 90/90 switches · half-kneeling psoas stretch · calf wall stretch · arch doming
🏃 10-min post-run: couch stretch · pigeon · both calf stretches · eccentric heel drops · plantar ball roll
🌙 15-min evening: the full list below, one body part at a time, top to bottom
🔙 Back
Stretch daily:
• Cat-cow — 10 slow reps (morning stiffness breaker)
• Child's pose — hold 60s, breathe into the low back
• Cobra / press-up — 10 reps, gentle, no pain
• Thread-the-needle — 30s per side (mid-back rotation)
Strengthen (pick 2, alternate days):
• Bird-dog — 3×10 per side, slow and square hips
• Glute bridge — 3×12 (bridges spare the low back by waking up glutes)
• Dead bug — 3×10 per side, low back pressed flat
🦴 Hips
Stretch daily:
• 90/90 switches — 10 slow transitions per side
• Pigeon pose — 60s per side
• Butterfly — 60s, tall spine
• World's greatest stretch — 5 per side (great pre-workout)
Strengthen (pick 2, alternate days):
• Clamshells w/ band — 3×15 per side
• Side-lying leg raise — 3×12 per side
• Banded monster walks — 3×10 steps each way (pairs with your glute program days)
🎯 Psoas (hip flexor)
Stretch daily:
• Half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch — 45–60s per side. Squeeze the back-leg glute and tuck the pelvis first; you should feel it in the front of the hip, not the low back
• Couch stretch — 30–45s per side (rear foot up on the couch/wall)
Strengthen (daily, it's low-load):
• Banded psoas march — 3×10 per side, slow
• Standing knee-drive isometric — press the knee up against your hands, 3×20s per side
• Hanging knee raise (pull-up bar) — 2×8, controlled, no swing
🦵 Calves (Achilles history — go slow, never bounce)
Stretch daily:
• Straight-knee wall stretch — 45s per side (gastroc)
• Bent-knee wall stretch — 45s per side (soleus — the one that guards your Achilles)
• Downward-dog pedals — 30s, gentle
Strengthen (every other day):
• Eccentric heel drops off a step — 3×10 per side, 3-second lower — the gold standard for Achilles resilience
• Straight-leg calf raises — 3×15
• Seated / bent-knee calf raises — 3×15 (hits soleus directly)
🦶 Feet
Stretch daily:
• Plantar-fascia ball roll — 60s per foot (lacrosse/golf ball, morning is best)
• Toes-up wall stretch — 30s per side
• Big-toe pulls & circles — 30s per foot
Strengthen (daily, takes 3 minutes):
• Short-foot / arch doming — 3×10 per foot
• Towel scrunches — 2×15 per foot
• Toe yoga (big toe down, little toes up, then flip) — 10 reps
• Single-leg balance — 3×30s per side (barefoot; strengthens foot + protects the ankle chain)
Stretches: daily, to mild tension only — never pain. Strength work: hips/calves every other day, psoas/feet daily. Skip anything that aggravates the Achilles and stay with controlled tempo. Not medical advice — if something hurts sharply, get it looked at.
